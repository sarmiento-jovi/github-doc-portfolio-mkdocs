# Credit Amendment Processes
This subsection defines the requirements governing the amendment or update of previously submitted or approved credit applications. Operational scenarios may arise where information associated with an application must be corrected, updated, or revised after the initial submission or approval.

The platform supports a controlled amendment process that allows authorized tenant users to submit update requests while ensuring that such changes undergo appropriate review and authorization. This process ensures that modifications to credit records are tracked, validated, and approved in accordance with the organization's credit governance policies.

*Credit Amendment Processes*

No. | Business Requirement | Definition
--- | --- | ---
BR-6.5-01 | Amendment Request Initiation | The system shall allow authorized tenant users to initiate a credit amendment request for an existing application record.
BR-6.5-02 | Amendment Eligibility Validation | The system shall validate whether the application is eligible for amendment based on defined businessrules.
BR-6.5-03 | Submission of Updated Information | The system shall allow users to provide updated information or documentation as part of the amendment request.
BR-6.5-04 | Amendment Workflow Routing | The system shall route amendment requests through the configured approval workflow based on the tenant organization’s approval hierarchy.
BR-6.5-05 | Amendment Approval Decision | The system shall allow authorized approvers to approve or reject amendment requests.
BR-6.5-06 | Amendment Status Tracking | The system shall maintain a status indicator reflecting the progress of the amendment request throughout the review process.
BR-6.5-07 | Amendment History Recording | The system shall record amendment actions and decisions associated with an application to maintain a complete audit history.
