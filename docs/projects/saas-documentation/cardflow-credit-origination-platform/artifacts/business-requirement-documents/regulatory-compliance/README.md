# Regulatory and Compliance Considerations
Financial institutions operate within regulated environments where credit decisions, customer information, and financial records must be handled in accordance with applicable regulatory and governance requirements. The **CardFlow Credit Origination Platform** must therefore support operational controls that enable tenant organizations to maintain compliance with relevant financial regulations, internal policies, and data protection standards.

The platform provides mechanisms that promote transparency, traceability, and responsible handling of sensitive customer information throughout the credit application lifecycle.
