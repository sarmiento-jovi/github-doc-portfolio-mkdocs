# KYC/AML Integration Considerations
This subsection highlights how the platform supports tenant organizations in performing customer verification activities that may be required as part of credit evaluation processes.

- Financial institutions may require verification of customer identity and supporting documentation as part of the credit application evaluation process.  
The platform supports the submission and review of documentation that may be used to assist tenant organizations in performing Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) verification activities.
- Tenant organizations may incorporate these verification activities into their internal credit review workflows as required by their operational policies.
