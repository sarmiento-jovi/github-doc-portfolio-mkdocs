# Data Protection and Privacy
This subsection outlines considerations related to the protection, access control, and secure handling of applicant data processed within the platform.

- The platform supports the responsible handling of applicant information and sensitive financial data in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.  
Access to customer data is restricted to authorized users, and the platform enforces role-based access controls to prevent unauthorized access to personal or financial information.
- The platform maintains secure storage of documents and information submitted as part of credit applications, ensuring that sensitive data is accessible only to users with appropriate permissions.
