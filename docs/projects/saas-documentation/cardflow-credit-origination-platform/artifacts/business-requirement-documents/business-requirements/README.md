# Business Requirements
This section defines the business requirements for the **CardFlow Credit Origination Platform**.

The requirements describe the expected system capabilities and operational behaviors necessary to support the credit card application lifecycle within tenant organizations.

The requirements are organized into functional areas aligned with the key operational processes introduced in the **[High-Level Business Process Overview](../business-process-overview/README.md)** section. Each functional area represents a group of related capabilities that support specific stages of the credit origination workflow, including application submission, document management, review and approval routing, and administrative configuration.

These requirements establish the functional expectations of the platform and provide the foundation for subsequent system design, development, and testing activities.
