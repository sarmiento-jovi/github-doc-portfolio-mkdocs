# Reporting and Audit Capabilities
This subsection defines the requirements for reporting and audit-related capabilities in the
**CardFlow Credit Origination Platform**:

- **Reporting** functions allow tenant users to monitor application activity, review application status, and generate operational reports to support credit management activities.
- **Audit** capabilities ensure that all actions performed within the platform are recorded and traceable. Maintaining a complete audit trail supports operational transparency, internal governance, and regulatory compliance requirements associated with credit decision processes.

*Reporting and Audit Capabilities*

No. | Business Requirement | Definition
--- | --- | --- 
BR-6.7-01 | Application Listing | The system shall provide a listing of credit applications accessible to authorized tenant users.
BR-6.7-02 | Application Filtering | The system shall allow users to filter application records based on predefined criteria such as application status, submission date, or applicant information.
BR-6.7-03 | Application Detail Viewing | The system shall allow authorized users to view detailed information associated with individual credit applications.
BR-6.7-04 | Report Export Capability | The system shall allow users to export application data for reporting and analysis purposes.
BR-6.7-05 | Approval History Visibility | The system shall display the history of review and approval actions associated with each application.
BR-6.7-06 | Audit Log Recording | The system shall record user activities and workflow actions performed within the platform.
BR-6.7-07 | Audit Log Accessibility | The system shall allow authorized administrative users to access audit logs for monitoring and investigation purposes.
