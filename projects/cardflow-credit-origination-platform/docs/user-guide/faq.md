# Frequently Asked Questions
This section addresses common questions related to the use of **CardFlow Credit Origination Platform**.

- **How can I check the status of my application?**

    Navigate to Applications or Requests and review the Current Status column. You may also open the application to view detailed workflow progress.

- **What does the Returned status mean?**

    Returned indicates that additional information, supporting documentation, or corrections are required before processing can continue.

- **Can I modify an application after submission?**

    Submitted applications generally cannot be edited unless they are returned for correction or an authorized amendment process is initiated.

- **How can I view workflow history?**

    Open the application and review the Workflow History section to view status changes, user actions, and processing milestones.

- **Can I download application information?**

    Yes. Authorized users may export application, workflow, approval, and audit information using the Reports module.

- **Why am I unable to approve a request?**

    Possible reasons include:
    
    - Required validations are incomplete
    - Supporting documents are missing
    - Approval prerequisites have not been satisfied
    - You do not have approval permissions
    - Review the approval requirements checklist for additional details.

- **Who can view audit history?**

    Audit history is typically available only to authorized users such as reviewers, approvers, administrators, auditors, or compliance personnel, depending on assigned permissions.


- **How do I change my notification preferences?**

    Navigate to Notifications and update the notification channels and event types that you wish to receive.
