# Data Retention
This subsection discusses considerations related to the storage and retention of application records, documents, and system activity logs for governance and audit purposes.

- The platform maintains records of submitted applications, supporting documents, and audit logs for reference and compliance purposes.  
Retained records allow tenant organizations to review historical application activity and maintain documentation required for audit and regulatory review.
- Retention policies may be defined according to the tenant organization's internal governance policies and applicable regulatory requirements.
