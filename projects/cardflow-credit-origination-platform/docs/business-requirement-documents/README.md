# Business Requirement Document

The **CardFlow Credit Origination Platform Business Requirement Document** defines the business and operational requirements for CardFlow, a multi-tenant SaaS solution designed to automate credit card application and approval processes.

The document establishes the business context, scope, stakeholder expectations, operational workflows, and business requirements that guide the design and implementation of the platform.
