# Current Operational Challenges
Financial institutions managing credit card applications often rely on semi-manual processes, including email-based document exchanges, spreadsheet tracking, and manual
approval routing. These practices introduce operational inefficiencies and control risks.

Common challenges include:

- Manual validation of submitted documents
- Delayed routing of applications across approval hierarchies
- Limited visibility into application status
- Inconsistent enforcement of approval thresholds
- Lack of centralized audit tracking
- Dependency on email-based communication
- Difficulty enforcing single active application rules

These processes increase turnaround time, elevate operational risk, and reduce transparency across credit review functions.
