# Business Objectives
The primary business objectives for implementing the **CardFlow Credit Origination Platform** include:

- Standardization of credit application workflows
- Reduction of manual intervention in routing and tracking
- Improved visibility into application lifecycle stages
- Centralized management of approval matrices
- Enforcement of business rules governing eligibility and submission
- Structured documentation and audit logging
- Support for configurable, tenant-specific approval hierarchies

By digitizing and automating the credit origination process, financial institutions can improve operational efficiency while maintaining governance and control.
